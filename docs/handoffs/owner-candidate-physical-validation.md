## Physical scenarios to qualify

Use **synthetic profiles only** (never real personal data) on the owner's chosen supported OS/device coverage. The package does not require a fleet or special hardware. For each case record: setup, exact steps, expected result, observed result, the candidate/source identity (archive + build), and the relevant **redacted** diagnostics (the paired JSON/Markdown export, never the raw personal profile). Device observations remain distinct from simulator evidence.

1. **Local generation / repeatability.** Create a synthetic profile, generate, and repeat: the canonical glyph, its visible signature hash, and the readable record are identical across runs and relaunches.
2. **Launch / relaunch.** Relaunch the app; saved synthetic records reopen with the same canonical glyph and record digest.
3. **Protected storage locked / unlocked.** Verify protected local records (the AES-GCM store) are accessible when unlocked and fail closed (or are unavailable) when locked, per the Keychain accessibility; a failed save is reported as a failure, never a success.
4. **Key availability.** Confirm the Keychain-backed keys are available where expected and that a missing key fails closed (record load returns not-found/invalid, never a decoded plaintext).
5. **Migration / interrupted save.** Interrupt a save mid-write (e.g., terminate the app); the last-verified record is preserved (atomic replace), and a subsequent save recovers cleanly.
6. **App termination during generation / export.** Terminate during the generation journey or during an export; no partial or corrupt record is written, and the temp export file is cleaned up (or is harmless and removed on next launch).
7. **Resource pressure.** Exercise generation and export under low-memory / disk-pressure conditions (simulated where practical); bounded diagnostic capture drops and accounts for overflow rather than growing unbounded.
8. **Display quality and responsiveness.** The canonical glyph, Flare previews, and results render correctly and remain responsive on the target device and OS.
9. **Native geocoding disclosure / offline fallback.** If birth-location geocoding is exercised, confirm the Apple system-service disclosure is presented and the offline/manual fallback works without a network.
10. **Native sharing / cancellation.** User-selected export/share presents the native share sheet; canceling or re-exporting repeatedly does not leak temp files or corrupt the intended artifact.
11. **No unexpected data transfer.** Confirm no network, CloudKit, sync, analytics, or upload path is exercised (the product's no-backend boundary).
